2. Describe your asset

Complete the asset fields to calculate its metadata hash.

Supply is minted once, at creation. The description and metadata are included in the hash recorded by the token. Keep the downloaded asset record to save them in PostgreSQL.

3. Read from the blockchain

Load saved asset retrieves its description and metadata from PostgreSQL, then reads balances and transfers from its token contract.

Look up an asset first, then run the simulation. No wallet popup is expected.

Simulates mint(address,uint256) from the connected wallet. No transaction is sent and no gas is spent. This contract exposes no additional mint function, so every caller should be rejected.

4. Transfer tokens

Connect your wallet and look up an asset to transfer its tokens.

Your connected wallet signs the transfer and pays network gas. Enter token amounts, such as 10000 or 2.5.
